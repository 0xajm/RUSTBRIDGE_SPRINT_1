Record real commands, authored PRs/commits, observations and limitations. Starter tests are not proof of completed merchant features.

A non-author performs the final handover. Assigned owners promote documentation; others review or reproduce it inside the same shared budget. Reassign unavailable owners, preserving authorship. These records are not a fixed pair schedule or private absence/grade log.

Individual case studies and extensions remain attributable to their authors, not prefilled shared answers. The common product does not require every student's extension.
